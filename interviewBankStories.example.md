# Interview story bank

One story per `## Sxx — Title` section, in STAR format. `Tags` help match stories to application and interview questions.
New stories drafted by the assistant are appended here only after you approve them.

## S01 — Rebuilding the merchant dashboard
Tags: leadership, performance, migration, stakeholder management
**Situation:** The merchant dashboard took 6+ seconds to load and merchants complained in every quarterly survey.
**Task:** As tech lead, I had to make it fast without pausing feature work for 4 teams.
**Action:** I proposed an incremental Next.js migration route by route, set performance budgets in CI, and paired with each team on their first migrated page.
**Result:** Load time dropped by 45% in 5 months, complaints about speed disappeared from the next survey, and no feature release slipped.

## S02 — Disagreeing with a product decision
Tags: conflict, communication, data-driven
**Situation:** Product wanted to add a mandatory sign-up step before checkout.
**Task:** I believed it would hurt conversion and needed to make the case without blocking the team.
**Action:** I suggested an A/B test instead of a debate, built both variants in two days, and agreed on the success metric with the product manager upfront.
**Result:** The mandatory step lowered conversion by 6%, we shipped the optional variant, and A/B tests became our default for checkout changes.
